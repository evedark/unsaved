# Color palette combinations for clean product posts

**Central idea:** A small palette creates a stronger product look than a broad brand board. The useful pattern is neutral base, strong text, one accent, and restrained surfaces.

## Breakdown
- **Neutral base:** Off-white, bone, or pale gray keeps the post lightweight.
- **Text color:** Near-black gives enough authority without feeling heavy.
- **Accent:** One green, blue, or red can mark the key term or system state.
- **Surface color:** Light gray cards help show inputs, outputs, and steps.
- **Avoid:** Too many gradients, soft luxury textures, and decorative colors that do not encode meaning.

## Why this matters
This supports a public product repo launch by making screenshots, carousels, and diagrams feel like one system.
